Main findings
  • Closed-book leader: gpt-5.6-sol·nr at 52.7%. Best Russian model: GigaChat-3-Ultra at 44.9%, nearly free on its freemium quota.
  • No model is production-ready without context: the best mean score across all questions is 52.7%. Real use needs retrieval over actual legal documents.
  • Judges flag answers as illegal far less often than as factually wrong. The real risk is confident advice citing norms that do not exist.
  • Reasoning adds ~2–3 points for 2–4× the tokens. The noreason variants are the cost-efficient pick.
